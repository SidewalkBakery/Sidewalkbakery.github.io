Our Story

Born in Austin.
Baked with intention.

We're not a brand. We're two friends with a kitchen, a dream, and a deeply unreasonable belief that your dog deserves the same quality you'd feed yourself at the farmers market.

Every batch is hand-rolled, hand-cut, and hand-delivered — literally — to every person with a dog we meet downtown. Free goodie bags with treats baked with love, a card with a QR code, and a whole lot of "oh my god your dog is gorgeous" energy.

"Keep Austin Weird" applies to dog treats too, y'all.
